# GuitarPal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-18

GuitarPal respects your privacy.

This app does not require user accounts and does not collect personally
identifiable information.

## Information We Do Not Collect

- No names, email addresses, or account information
- No location data
- No contacts or photos
- No advertising identifiers
- No third-party analytics services

## Local Data

All practice data (such as practice sessions, exercises, and statistics)
is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any servers.

## Third-Party Services

GuitarPal does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated in the future if app functionality changes.
Any updates will be reflected on this page.
